Dear VGZT community,

We’re excited to share that Virtual Gastrulation Zoom Talks Season 8 is now officially up and running!

Thank you for being part of the VGZT community in previous seasons. We’re delighted to welcome you back for another season of conversations across developmental biology.

This year, we have an exciting line-up of researchers joining us to discuss their work, scientific journeys, and perspectives across gastrulation, morphogenesis, stem cells, embryonic development, organoids, and related areas.

We’ve attached our Season 8 poster with the current speaker line-up. Please note that the listed dates and times are tentative while we coordinate schedules across different time zones. Confirmed details for individual talks will be announced ahead of each session.

We’re also especially excited about opportunities for students and early-career researchers this season.

🔬 Submit an abstract
If you are a student or early-career researcher and would like to showcase your work, we invite you to submit an abstract for the opportunity to present to the VGZT community.

🏆 Presentation Awards
Season 8 will also include awards recognizing outstanding presentations.

We’re also happy to introduce our Season 8 organizing team, who have been working behind the scenes to put together this year’s program and create more opportunities for interaction within the developmental biology community.

🌐 Season 8 website: https://vgzt.org/
📝 Abstract submission: https://vgzt.org/abstracts/

We hope you’ll join us again this season, and we look forward to seeing both familiar and new faces at VGZT Season 8!

Best,
VGZT Season 8 Organizing Team
